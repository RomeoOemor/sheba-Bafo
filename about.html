Our Story

We started with one goal: to bring back real, home-style cooking in a fast-paced world.

Who We Are

Born in South Africa.
Made with Love.

Mpura Stews was born from a simple frustration: why is it so hard to find real, home-cooked food when you're busy? We grew up on meals that took hours to prepare — rich beef stews, aromatic chicken pots, hearty vegetable dishes passed down through generations.

We took those family recipes — from Limpopo kitchens, Cape Malay traditions, and township braai culture — and made them accessible to everyone. Every batch is freshly prepared in small quantities, vacuum-sealed, and delivered to your door or local store.

This isn't factory food. This is food with a story, made by people who care.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide convenient, nutritious meals without compromising on taste. We believe every South African deserves access to real food — not processed packets full of chemicals, but meals that nourish the body and warm the soul.
